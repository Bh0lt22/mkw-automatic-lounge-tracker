Track scores manually, without a capture card. Pick the players position by position.

  • 1. Paste the 12-player roster, just like in the main mode.
  • 2. Click Save scores under any race column.
  • 3. Pick each player one by one (1st place, 2nd place, …). Mark DC for disconnections on the last two spots.
  • 4. Export scores when done, same as the main mode.
